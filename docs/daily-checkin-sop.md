# U-Flow SOP: Daily Morning Check-In

Purpose: Daily routine to review active client performance and decide where to focus for the day.

## Step 1: Open Meta Ads Manager
Check overall account health: spend pacing, delivery status, anything flagged (disapprovals, learning phase issues).

## Step 2: Review each active client account
Go through every active client account one by one (currently: T Plumbers, Leo Tiles, Bizxcel, add new ones here as they come on).

For each account, check:
- New leads / messages received (Leads Center, WhatsApp)
- Cost per lead and spend vs budget
- Ad delivery status
- Anything flagged from recent call notes, if applicable

Escalation trigger: No new outreach or contacts in 3-4 days on any account. Flag it and deal with it that same day, ahead of everything else below.

(Note: the exact metrics list above is a best guess. Confirm or edit this list after a few days of use.)

## Step 3: Follow up with clients directly
For each client:
- Confirm they've received what was delivered and are executing on it
- Check if anything's come up on their end
- Note what's still outstanding to deliver or follow up on

Steps 2 and 3 don't run in a fixed order. It depends on what's come in overnight.

(Assuming this happens over WhatsApp by default, since that's the SA-side channel. Confirm if some clients get a call or email instead.)

## Step 4: Decide the next play
Once the check-in is done:
- If Step 2 flagged an escalation, deal with that first.
- If everything's healthy, choose between:
  - Sales/outreach work: pushing to bring in new clients
  - Build/product work: CRM, automation, PlumbFlow, etc.

Since it's just one person handling both sides right now, this is a prioritization call based on what needs attention most that day, not a delegation decision.
